COLLAPSE IMMINENT: TWELVE PERIGEES REMAINING

Protocol OURO-80R05 is a game set in a bleak, distant, and dark future of humanity. All things must come to an end, and the sun is next to die in the grand scheme of the merciless universe.

After a vainglorious attempt by the engineers, architects, and solar physicists of NEXUS Station to prolong its life, Protocol OURO-80R05 is brought into full effect: abusing the time-warping nature of black holes to go back just before the sun collapsed. In theory, the Protocol is infinite. But time can only be bought for so long before the past catches up with the present.

The clock is ticking. The odds are near-impossible. You are humanity's final hope.
